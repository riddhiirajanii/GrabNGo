| Canvas Block | Details |
|---|---|
| **1. Problem** | • Long queues during busy hours (lunch & evening peak).<br>• High rate of food order mix-ups and missing items.<br>• Students do not know when their food will be ready.<br>• *Existing Alternatives:* Ordering physically at counter, waiting in line, manual phone calls. |
| **2. Customer Segments** | • **Target Users:** IIT Patna students, faculty, and campus residents.<br>• **Early Adopters:** Students with tight class/lab breaks and frequent food court visitors.<br>• **Partners/Buyers:** Campus food court vendors & outlet owners (Angeethi Café, Arya Services, etc.). |
| **3. Unique Value Proposition** | **"Order Smart. Track Live. Save Time."**<br>A unified campus food court platform allowing students to pre-order meals in advance, monitor live queue status, track preparation progress, and pick up food instantly when ready without standing in lines. |
| **4. Solution** | • Advance pre-ordering with scheduled pickup slots.<br>• Real-time queue transparency and estimated ready times.<br>• Unique alphanumeric pickup tokens (e.g. #A-104) to eliminate confusion and wrong handovers. |
| **5. Channels** | • IIT Patna student WhatsApp and Telegram groups.<br>• Campus community forums and social media.<br>• Physical QR codes and table stands at food court vendor stalls.<br>• Direct onboarding partnerships with outlet owners. |
| **6. Revenue Streams** | • Agreed micro-commission percentage per order from vendors.<br>• Modest nominal convenience fee per pre-order.<br>• Tiered monthly student subscription pass ("Zero Convenience Fee Club"). |
| **7. Cost Structure** | • Web and mobile app development & maintenance.<br>• Cloud server hosting, database, and real-time WebSocket infrastructure.<br>• Payment gateway transaction processing charges.<br>• Vendor onboarding, tablet hardware assistance (KDS), and marketing. |
| **8. Key Metrics** | • Number of registered active students and daily order volume.<br>• Average counter waiting time saved per student.<br>• Order accuracy rate (reduction in mix-ups / incorrect items).<br>• Vendor throughput and peak hour fulfillment capacity. |
| **9. Unfair Advantage** | • Direct on-ground access to IIT Patna student community.<br>• Deep, first-hand understanding of campus dining schedules, break timings, and food court pain points.<br>• Close relationships with local campus vendors. |
