# Grab n Go — Problem Statement & Research Findings

## Context: IIT Patna Food Court & Campus Outlets
Ordering food across campus dining outlets (including the central Food Court and Angeethi Café) is currently heavily manual, uncertain, and time-consuming. During short breaks between academic classes, laboratory sessions, and evening rush hours, students experience excessive delays trying to purchase meals.

---

## 1. Observed Fieldwork & Evidence
* **Long & Unpredictable Queues:** Intense rushes during lunch and evening peak hours create long, slow-moving lines.
* **Zero Visibility Across Outlets:** Students cannot gauge crowd levels or wait times at alternative outlets until they physically reach the location.
* **Squandered Break Times:** Breaks are consumed standing in queues, resulting in students skipping meals or arriving late to classes/labs.
* **Post-Order Waiting & Confusion:** Even after ordering manually, students have to linger around counters with no certainty on preparation status or queue position.
* **Order Errors & Mix-ups:** Verbal communication and paper receipts lead to forgotten items, incorrect deliverables, and payment friction.

---

## 2. Voice of the Student (Field Interviews)
> *"Sometimes the queue is so long that I just skip ordering because I never know how long I’ll have to wait."*

> *"My whole break sometimes goes into getting food, and I’ve even been late to class because I was waiting for my order."*

> *"Even after ordering, I don't know when my food will actually be ready, I wish I could see which outlet is faster before ordering."*

---

## 3. The As-Is Workflow vs. The To-Be Workflow

### As-Is (Manual Current State):
```
[Call / Visit Counter] 
        ↓
[Communicate Order Verbally] (Prone to errors, noise, missing items)
        ↓
[Payment Hassle] (Manual UPI/Cash, queuing at register)
        ↓
[Uncertain Waiting] (No queue position, standing in crowd)
        ↓
[Repeatedly Inquire Status] ("Bhaiya, mera order hua?")
        ↓
[Collect Food] (Risk of wrong packet handover)
```

### To-Be (Grab n Go Solution):
```
[Browse Campus Outlets & Live Queue Indicators]
        ↓
[Explore Menu & Place Advance Pre-Order]
        ↓
[Seamless Digital Payment / Subscription Pass]
        ↓
[Receive Unique Pickup Token (e.g., #A-104)]
        ↓
[Live In-App Order Tracking & ETA Countdown]
        ↓
[Notification Alert: "Food is Ready for Pickup!"]
        ↓
[Express Counter Grab & Go]
```

---

## 4. Key Business Objectives
1. **Reduce Wait Time:** Slash average counter waiting time from 20–30 minutes to under 2 minutes (just grab and go).
2. **Eliminate Order Mix-Ups:** Standardize digitized orders with unique alphanumeric pickup tokens.
3. **Queue Transparency:** Provide campus-wide visibility into real-time rush and prep times so students can make informed dining decisions.
